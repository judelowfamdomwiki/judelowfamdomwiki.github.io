Overview

JudeLow (also written JudeLosasso, and in-game simply as Jude or Judy) is a Minecraft content creator and one of the defining trap-builders of the Strength SMP community. Where most Strength SMP players built bases, grinded Strength, or chased kills, JudeLow's identity on the server is built around trapping: ambush pits, redstone kill-loops, boat and elytra farms, and the psychological bait that gets a target to stand exactly where they need to be.

He entered Season 4 as a newcomer and immediately made an impact — the Season 4 page records him as taking the first kill of the season during the grace period. Across Season 4 he was placed in Team 100 with Rejoicin and Fymada, then formed the Deathtrap Duo with Conexion (Sharpness) — a partnership that defined the season and ended in betrayal, an event that became the emotional core of his most-viewed content.

Across the Season 4 cycle he accumulated a substantial body of work: eleven titled Season 4 videos — the last published five days after the season closed — roughly 5.9M views on the single most famous of them, a permanent monument built in his honour at Strength SMP, and a community reputation that had climbed from a C-tier at the start of the season to A tier for PvP, S tier for "hardest-to-kill," and top-tier "AURA" by season's end.

The latest documented chapter is Strength Trials (4–20 September 2026), the vetting server between Seasons 4 and 5, where JudeLow ran solo, briefly held the server's Monarch crown, and was permanently banned from the event by the Ban Hammer — but is confirmed returning for Season 5.

Early History

Before Strength SMP: the trapper who was already famous

JudeLow did not discover Minecraft PvP on Strength SMP. He arrived with a reputation. The Money SMP page documents him winning a tournament in Mini Season 2 and earning over $100 for his team with traps alone, and the MrBeast storyline on the Strength SMP wiki shows him being recognised — and targeted — by one of the biggest creators on the platform. The Money SMP page also credits his trapping in "Money SMP 24/7" and "Mr. Beast Challenges", not just on Strength SMP.

The MrBeast arc — from first victim to last

This is the clearest documented piece of JudeLow's real-world reputation arc, and it is a genuine comeback story. The Strength SMP wiki itself summarises it as a "comeback of the decade"; the details below come from the MrBeast video and from JudeLow's own documentary of the sequel.

Round 1 — the loss (23 May 2026). In MrBeast Gaming's "10 YouTubers vs 1 Secret Traitor" (link), JudeLow was the very first player eliminated. This is confirmed by MrBeast's own video transcript: another contestant says "I saw Jude go off on his own" and "I see Jude layin' on the ground, DEAD." MrBeast (playing as "Jimmy") was the secret traitor. Jude's own team-mates then turned on each other over the body — one concluded "I did kill Judelo at the start." Jude was gone from the game before it properly began.

Round 2 — the comeback (July 2026). JudeLow then competed in the follow-up, "10 YouTubers vs 2 Secret Traitors," where he was one of the secret traitors himself — and he killed MrBeast, who was the last victim of the event. He documented it himself in the video "Betraying MrBeast in his own event" (27 July 2026, link), and won the event's prize: a real diamond sword worth $10,000. A Shorts upload on 6 September 2026 is titled "how i outsmarted 500 people for $10,000."

The story's final beat is a direct, verified quote from that video — and it is the most on-brand line in his entire catalogue:

"You can't trap the trapper, Mr. Beast." — JudeLow, "Betraying MrBeast in his own event" (27 July 2026)

The context matters: Jude had just noticed MrBeast attempting to trap him, and called it out before it landed. That single line is the thesis of his Strength SMP career — the trapper is the one who sees the trap.

⚠️ This section contains a genuine, unresolved contradiction in the source material.

What Season 3.5 was. Per the Season 3.5 wiki page, Season 3.5 was a mini-season run between Season 3 and Season 4. Its dates are given as started July 2025, ended July 2025, and its owner was _Tai. The contradiction. The wiki disagrees with itself on when JudeLow first appeared:

Any account of JudeLow's Season 3.5 activity beyond "he was listed as a guest" is currently unsourced. Treat it as a genuine blank, not a hidden story. His first documented action on Strength SMP is Season 4. And it was immediate: the Season 4 page records first kill = JudeLow (Grace Period) — he took the first kill of Season 4, inside the pre-competition grace period. Season 4 ran 13 September 2025 – 23 August 2026. Owners: _Tai (Owner) and Mugm_ (Former Co-Owner). The scoring system is the key to reading everything else: "Whenever you kill a player, you gain +1 strength… Whenever you die, you lose -1 strength. This season's strength cap is +5, and the lowest strength is +1. Once you're at +1 strength, you don't lose strength when dying. You can craft up to +3 strength."

JudeLow's arc over those eleven months can be read as four movements: the Team 100 outcast → the trapper's revenge → the Deathtrap partnership and its betrayal → the solo king of traps.

Movement I — The bow at spawn, and the revenge that built a reputation

Season 4 opened with a trio: Sharpness (known in-game as Conexion), Evourai and Spongs. Per the wiki, they "decide to team despite the massive differences in PvP skill." They found a Trial Chamber, died repeatedly inside it, and came away with two maces. They had a friendly interaction with Falconu of The Pirates. Then they went looking for a village.

And JudeLow shot them with a bow. Per Conexion/Season 4:

"As the three are trying to find a village, JudeLow appears and starts shooting at them with a bow. Since they lacked proper gear, they decided to run and barely managed to escape."

This is the first documented JudeLow act on the server: an unprovoked ranged attack on three undergeared players. It established him immediately as the server's aggressor.

The U_ltra incident. Escaping, the trio realised they needed better gear. Sharpness and Spongs sent a happy ghast to scout while Evourai went out on foot; Evourai tried to kill the undergeared U_ltra and was instead attacked by Twirps and Dol9hin. Sharpness and Spongs came to help. All three died and lost their Strength.

The Fymada incident — the event that made them enemies. Desperate for Strength, Sharpness and Evourai "decide to camp in Fymada and Shandrea's base and kill them." They succeeded — and then discovered their victims were both at +1 Strength, meaning neither dropped anything. Per the wiki, both felt terrible, apologised, and returned their items before leaving.

JudeLow's response: a turtle farm trap. "Jude traps Sharpness out of revenge for killing his teammate Fymada but ultimately gives him back his gear after hearing that Sharpness and Evourai had apologized and given Fymada her stuff back." The wiki adds a detail: Sharpness "didn't know how to obtain turtle scutes and initially slaughtered a couple of Jude's turtles before realizing" — and then fell into the trap.

And Sharpness did not accept it back. The wiki states flatly: "Despite saying he did, Sharpness did not actually forgive Jude after the latter gave him back his stuff."

⚠️ Source caveat. This entire sequence — including the apology, the forgiveness and the non-forgiveness — is narrated only on the Conexion/Season 4 page. There is no second source. Judging Sharpness' state of mind is a single-author claim and should be read as Sharpness' account, not as established fact.

The 4v1 that settled it. Sharpness and Evourai watched one of JudeLow's traps aimed at _Tai and ItsN00B backfire, found the trap, and chased him. The wiki's phrasing is "The four players then start chasing Jude in a 4v1 which led to Sharpness getting the kill and cheering" — note that the wiki names only Sharpness and Evourai but refers to "four players", so the full size of the group is not documented.

The trio dissolves. "Some point after this, Evourai became inactive and/or left Strength SMP to focus on personal projects. Sharpness was unhappy with Spongs skills and left to become a solo player."

Movement II — Deathtrap Duo: the partnership that defined the season

Sharpness solo "made a name for himself for being one of the most difficult members to kill on Strength SMP." He held +5 Strength and "frequently took on outnumbered fights only to win."

Critically, he also "starts using others' bases as his own for gear repair and other things" — because, in the wiki's words, of "his lack of his base." JudeLow saw the opening and set a turtle farm trap in his own base to catch Sharpness at it.

⚠️ A duplicated entry worth flagging. The turtle farm trap appears twice in the Conexion/Season 4 article: once in the Buddy Boys section (as Jude's revenge for Fymada) and again at the top of the Deathtrap Duo section (as Jude exploiting Sharpness's base-freeloading). The text of the two entries is nearly identical. Either the wiki has duplicated one event, or Jude built the trap twice. This biography treats it as one recurring build; the wiki does not resolve it.

The bargain. "After some bargaining, they two decide to team up" — Jude shows Sharpness his traps "so that he can use them even when he's not there", and "Sharpness trained Jude in PvP so that he doesn't have to rely on his traps so much." The wiki adds: "They felt invincible due to their complimenting synergy with one another."

The trade is the whole story. Jude gave Sharpness passive income from his traps; Sharpness gave Jude the ability to fight without hiding.

This is the mechanical heart of JudeLow's character development in Season 4. He began the season leaning almost entirely on traps and ranged attacks; he left it as a genuinely dangerous combatant who happened to also build traps. It is the single biggest arc on his wiki page.

They are the duo "Deathtrap Duo" — the Season 4 teams table lists them as Members with ParkerGameBrain as a former member — and part of the "Wager Trio" with _Tai.

Proof it worked — the Dol9hin/Nufuli fight. Feeling invincible, they picked a fight against Dol9hin and Nufuli. Jude killed Nufuli. ItsN00B and an invisible player joined in. LuigiToan arrived and used his Bow ultimate on Sharpness, "nearly killing him before Jude's quick reaction with a health pot saves him." The wiki records them cheering afterwards at having beaten "near impossible odds against powerful Strength players" — the high-water mark of the alliance.

The tournament run. They entered a 2v2 tournament and "make it to the semi-finals before they lost to Swizu and Sharkilz."

Movement III — The 8x8 trap, the 6x6 trap, and the betrayal

JudeLow told this story himself in "never trust your 'teammate'" (10 January 2026, ~5.9M views — his most-viewed Strength SMP video). The wiki's account and his own narration line up closely; where they differ, both are reported.

Recruitment. Jude explains he made a deal with Parker, who "recently got added to the SMP": "I would let him live in my base and I would give him gear on the terms that he would help me in sharpness trap players." The role split is the innovation — Sharpness baits, Parker pulls the lever, Jude films.

The 8x8 trap. The idea: "What if I waited until everyone logged off and then secretly built a gigantic 8x8 trap in the arena?" inside the coliseum hosting the tournament. They logged on at 1:00 in the morning when nobody was online and built for three hours. "After 3 hours of building, it was done."

It failed. Their opponents used invisibility, so Jude couldn't read nametags to confirm the trap was baited correctly. The wiki's summary is blunt: Sharpness choked, and the opponents "scared" of fighting Jude. Jude later conceded on camera that he'd been "being a gigantic [__] by hiding" and decided to "go die like a man."

The redemption arc — the 1v1 tournament. While spectating, a player dropped out of a 1v1 tournament and Jude jumped in. He explicitly abandoned traps: "I wasn't going to use any traps, so this 1v1 tournament would truly be a test to Sharpness's training." He beat Danny / Axel and then fought Swizu — "the player who had eliminated me earlier" — in the final. He won. On camera:

"The only reason I won this is cuz all of that training we did together, right? I had done it using Sharpness's training."

⚠️ Discrepancy to note. The wiki says Jude and Sharpness lost the 2v2 semi-final to Swizu and Sharkilz_. Jude's own account in the same video says Swizu knocked him out of a 1v1 earlier in the episode. These are likely two different tournament brackets, but the wiki does not distinguish them. Both are reported here.

The 6x6 trap — the one that worked. "Me and Parker spent a couple hours building a 6x6 trap in the middle of nowhere, and Parker would be on a lever pull this time." Sharpness baited it. Result: "he baited the trap perfectly, causing both players to get trapped in the same hole." Jude's own reaction: "We had done it."

⚠️ Ordering conflict between sources. Jude narrates making the Parker deal before the 8x8 trap. But the wiki's Conexion/Season 4 page places the 5-player team (Sharpness, Jude, DoomedCow, saparata, ParkerGameBrain) as forming after Jude and Sharpness were already teamed, and states that team "split when Jude pulled the backup trap on Sharpness." These two orderings contradict each other on when Parker entered the picture. Most likely both are partly right — a deal made early, formal team membership later — but the wiki does not say so.

The betrayal. "As Jude is showing Sharpness a back-up trap, Sharpness pulls it on him, claiming it was revenge for the turtle-trap kill."

But Jude had planned for it: "However, Jude had expected his betrayal and planned a backup trap in the case Sharpness did betray him. Sharpness falls and dies to potions of harming."

The aftermath — a temporary second alliance. "Shortly after the betrayal, DragonMC gets a glitched god bow which he uses to snipe Jude along with several others." After Jude respawned, "Sharpness shows up at his base to hide from Dragon and theorizes ways he could kill him." Then — "He later teams up with Jude again against Dragon and his teammates." The rivalry was not permanent; it was re-litigated.

The two events that came next:

  • The escape room challenge. "Sharpness takes part in Jude's escape room challenge, but unbeknownst to him, there was a trap and he dies." Sharpness got his revenge at the 20v2 event (everyone vs. Tai & Jude), "where he gets the kill on Jude."
  • The base demolition. Sharpness, Twirps, Arcn and Falconu formed a plan to "take control of Spawn away from trappers like JudeLow and prevent Tai from becoming king of the server, which was a longstanding goal of Twirps." Sharpness deviated. He "spawned two withers in Jude's base, blowing the original base up completely." The Season 4 POI list marks JudeLow's Base at Spawn as "(Blown up)" — a fate shared by The Stage, The Auction House (burned), the Farm Warehouse, The Fountain, The Bathrooms, the Pokecenter and others.

⚠️ The 20v2 and the escape room are documented only on Sharpness' page, and no date is given anywhere on the wiki for either. No dates have been invented for them here.

Movement IV — Solo, and the King of Traps

After the 5-player team split, Jude kept ParkerGameBrain and Sharpness kept DoomedCow and saparata. Saparata then went inactive, leaving Sharpness with just DoomedCow, and Sharpness later added Flankeee and Twirps. JudeLow was, from this point on, effectively the leader of the server's trap scene.

ParkerGameBrain is the key relationship here. Per ParkerGameBrain/Season 4, he "joined Strength SMP midway through, immediately teaming with JudeLow" and "is one of the 4 main trappers of the server" — a phrase DoomedCow's page also uses, with both pages naming JudeLow among the four. Their duo name is "Tick Duo", with Boomiee, godispase, Bitzel and Falconu listed as its allies.

By mid-to-late Season 4 the trap hierarchy was essentially JudeLow's, and the wiki says so directly in Conexion's history: the plan to take Spawn was explicitly about taking it "away from trappers like JudeLow." JudeLow was the reason the anti-trapper alliance formed at all.

In the Season 4 POI list, the enduring monuments are:

  • Judelow's Base (Bases section, Outskirts) — a surviving base, distinct from the Spawn-located one that Sharpness' withers blew up.
  • "JudeLow and _Tai 2v30 Statues" (Scattered Areas) — a permanent build in the world commemorating a JudeLow/_Tai two-versus-thirty engagement. Tai published a video titled "2 Trappers VS 20 Minecraft Pros" on 13 March 2026. ⚠️ The wiki does not confirm the statues commemorate that video specifically, and the strikethrough formatting on _Tai in the POI list is unexplained.

JudeLow's role, honestly assessed

JudeLow's entry is the fourth of nine holders in a chain that ran from late September 2025 to mid-May 2026 — and it is the only entry where the holder gave it straight back.

Every other handover in this chain involved a lie, a fake, a strategic reason, or a grudge:

  • conexion hid it with LeoExists_ "so nobody would suspect he had it."
  • Flowtives was handed it to fake-void it and deceive _Tai.
  • Twirps was given it because he "didn't want to hold the weight of it."

JudeLow killed _Tai with a trap, had the Dragon Egg in his hands, and returned it. That is the whole entry. No deception, no holdout, no play.

⚠️ Two things are genuinely missing and are not filled in here:

  1. No date is recorded for JudeLow's holding of the egg. The table's ordering implies it fell after _Tai's stint (27 Mar 2026) and before conexion's (6 May 2026) — so late March to early May 2026 — but the wiki states nothing, and the table is not in strict date order.
  2. No video documents it. None of JudeLow's eleven Season 4 videos is about the Dragon Egg. It appears to have been a genuine, unscripted server moment rather than content.

Aftermath: the egg's legacy shaped the next server

The Dragon Egg was not carried into Strength Trials. Instead, Trials introduced a new mechanic built from the same object: the Ban Hammer, "the only one craftable with the only Dragon Egg." Macing someone with it permanently banned them from the event.

JudeLow's Trials Dragon Egg involvement: he maced saidminion, "who picked up the Dragon Egg after the Ban Wave Ender Dragon died in the Overworld." The egg then passed to _Tai, conexion, sweatgod, and finally InProper, who "used it to craft the Ban Hammer."

And it was the Ban Hammer that ended JudeLow's Trials run. See below.

Major Conflicts

Who: JudeLow vs. Sharpness, Evourai, Spongs. What: JudeLow opened the season by shooting three undergeared players with a bow and driving them off. No prior grievance is documented. Personality and Playstyle

The trapper's dilemma

Everything about JudeLow's Strength SMP identity follows from one structural fact. In Season 4, "whenever you kill a player, you gain +1 strength" and the cap is +5 — a kill is worth exactly the same +1 whether it comes from a clean sword duel or from a three-hour redstone build. For a player who farms Strength through raw PvP, that is a numbers game. For a trapper, it is a completely different proposition: the only way to beat a fighter on that board is to make each kill cost less risk, less time, and more humiliation than a straight fight would. The 6×6 trap isn't a better +1. It's theatre.

The pivot: from trap-dependence to competence

The most important character beat in the wiki is a technical one, and it's easy to miss because it's buried in a paragraph.

Sharpness's contribution to JudeLow wasn't friendship — it was mechanical independence. Sharpness trained him in PvP "so that he doesn't have to rely on his traps so much." JudeLow's Season 4 lowest strength is 3, two steps above the server's +1 floor. On Tai's community "Hardest-to-kill" list he shares S tier with conexion and ItsN00B, the only other players in that tier. On the community Season 5 PvP list he is A tier, and Tai's Top 10 Active PvPers puts him 8th.

He arrived as a player who leaned almost entirely on traps. He left as a player who could win in direct PvP and still build traps. That dual competence is his actual legacy — and it's why Sharpness' March 2026 video could credibly be titled "I Got Revenge on Minecraft's Greatest Trapper" and get 1.4 million views. A pure trapper is easy to write off. A trained fighter who builds traps is a problem.
